# D11: [F1]

Status: **approved (weak form); restated by D11′**

**[F1]**, for R1-live only. A fairness assumption on transient failures, **per call kind and per fd/listener**: a call that is attempted infinitely often succeeds (does not fail transiently) infinitely often. Transient failures may recur forever, but never refuse permanently. The stronger "finitely many failures" was rejected as unrealistic for a long-running server (occasional ENOMEM/ENOBUFS forever). Using the stronger form needs a fresh decision
