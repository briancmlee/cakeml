# D27: Interrupted close

Status: **decided (user, via coordinator)**

**Interrupted close** (async-spike's finding #2). An interrupted `close` (EINTR) may leave the fd open or free it; L1 forgets the fd at its close call either way, and the spec's episode ends only at a successful close (or fd reuse). So the close the echo owes after the peer's EOF is the *call*: `echo_live_unless`'s eof conjunct is "a close call on the episode's fd while the episode is open" (`l0_close_call`). Corollary `echo_live_unless_closed` (proved in L3-00): if no close call on an accepted fd fails (`close_calls_ok io`), the episode is closed (`ep_closed`, the earlier form). L3-00 c2b12a300
