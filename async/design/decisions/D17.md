Running out of fds at accept. **ENFILE is retried** (only EMFILE pauses). **While the process is out of fds, L1 retries accept after a backoff** of `pf_backoff` rounds (a model constant; no proof uses its value), as real servers pause accepting and retry, instead of waiting for a close. No "no spurious EMFILE" hypothesis. In the model: `pause` in the PF state; L1 polls the listener only when `pause = 0`.
