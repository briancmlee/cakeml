# D9: The L0 model is anchored on the standards (POSIX.1-2024, RFC 9293/1122), with ci

Status: **decided**

The L0 model is anchored on the standards (POSIX.1-2024, RFC 9293/1122), with cited sections. Where they are loose: nondeterminism. A platform behaviour they forbid is a documented extension [X]. The probes are conformance tests (§4.2–4.3)
