# D41: moonshot_live fixes: pending connects, transient connect failures, failed listens, EMFILE accepts

Status: **decided (user, 2026-10-17, via the desk; Q-10 + addendum)**. Amends the approved `moonshot_live` statement.

User's words, in order: "For 2, is (b) just like adding a similar fairness assumption? If so I think I’m okay to do
that over (a)…"; then "Let’s just fix bugs 1 and 3" (desk asked whether bug 2 was dropped or deferred); then,
to the orchestrator's revised recommendation below, "Yes".

Statement: `async/moonshot-live/moonshotLiveStmtsScript.sml` (spike/moonshot-live @7a07a6069; `hard_fail`,
`sys_excuse`, `moonshot_live`) and `async/l0sys-fair/l0sysFairScript.sml` (`F1_sys`). No trusted definitions change.

1. **A pending connect is not a hard failure.** `hard_fail` excludes EINPROGRESS. A pending connect then settles
   by the approved kernel fairness (handshake or observed connect failure). Strictly strengthens the promise.
2. **Transient failures of connect: F1 extended (option (b)), keyed by (process, target port).** "If process k
   attempts connects to port p infinitely often, infinitely often one returns a non-transient outcome"
   (EINTR/ENOBUFS/ENOMEM transient). Per-port, not per-fd, because each retry uses a new socket fd (a per-fd form
   would be vacuous). Same weak observed "i.o. ⇒ i.o." form as D11′; no new kind of trust; F4 (connects
   succeed) stays unassumed. A non-transient failure (e.g. ECONNREFUSED) remains a hard-error excuse.
   **Listen: any failed listen of ours is an observed excuse.** Programs call listen once and exit on any failure
   via the existing `l0_listen_failed` outcome; F1 on listen would be vacuous.
3. **EMFILE accept drops: observed excuse "a hard accept failure by some process"** (EAGAIN excluded). An accept
   failing with EMFILE may reset one queued connection inside the call (D17 `emfile_drops`), with no kernel
   Break label. The strong corollary covers it under its existing fd-limit launch hypotheses.

Rejected: for 2, (a) a headline excuse "connects/listens fail transiently infinitely often" plus a corollary.
First recommended by the orchestrator and the desk by analogy with D18, but D18 is about HARD errors; transient
errors are handled everywhere by assuming F1 (D11′), so (b) keeps one treatment (orchestrator withdrew its D18
citation). Retrying listen on transient errors plus F1: a program change; real servers exit on listen failure.
Leaving 2 unfixed: `moonshot_live` unprovable for any retrying client/proxy.
