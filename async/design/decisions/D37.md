# D37: Moonshot step-12 safety headline (middle isolation, connect episodes)

Status: **approved (user, 2026-10-17)**

**The moonshot step-12 safety headline is approved as stated** in `spike/moonshot-glue` @ 9fd52ab06,
`async/moonshot-glue/moonshotStep12StmtsScript.sml` (`moonshot_safe_stmt`).

- **Isolation: `isolated a b rs ss`, the middle option.** No outsider on ports a and b: no Env
  `EIncoming` on a/b; no Env `EConnUp`/`EArrive` on an end whose target is a/b; at-once connects to
  a/b are paired. Other ports, including the client's private c, may carry outside traffic.
- **Conclusion:** `kv_client_safe` over the client's CONNECT episodes only (`conn_only`), on
  `fromList (proc_events client_ix rs)`, in every
  `l0sys_iso_run (moonshot_sys cprog pprog kprog a b c N rqs) a b rs`.
- **B2** must give `b2_quiet_ab`: an unpaired episode on a/b reads nothing.
- **`no_close_stdio`** (never close fds 0–2) is approved for the client and the proxy.
- **Outcomes:** each claim allows `stdio_closed` (D33) and `l0_listen_failed` (fdok×3, then a failed
  listen).
- **Generic** in binaries satisfying `l0_client_claim` / `proxy_claim_safe` / `kv_claim_safe`.

Rejected alternatives: closed-system isolation (stronger than needed); design/45's ports-only
isolation with an unrestricted conclusion, which is false (an outsider on c could feed the client).
